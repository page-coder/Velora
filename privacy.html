Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary. Have it legally reviewed before launch.

Images

Images you process with the browser tools stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

Account data

If you sign up we store, via Supabase: email, display name, optional avatar (in the velora-images storage bucket), daily usage counts, and activity records (tool, file name, status, file sizes). Row-level security limits each record to its owner.

Third parties

Google Analytics measures traffic. Google Fonts serves the typeface. Google or Apple sign-in is used only if you choose it.

Your choices

Change your password or sign out other devices in Settings. To request deletion of your data, use the Contact page.
