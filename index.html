Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Compression, resizing, conversion and bulk optimization work for guests. An account adds a dashboard, history and tracked daily limits.

Are my images uploaded?

Not by the browser tools. They are processed locally with your browser’s canvas. Signed-in activity records store only the file name, sizes and tool, never the image.

Which formats are supported?

Input: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP (up to 80 MB). Output: JPEG and PNG everywhere, WebP and AVIF where your browser can encode them. JPEG XL cannot be encoded by browsers today.

What are the daily limits?

Limits are stored in the database and enforced by Supabase for signed-in users, and reset at midnight India Standard Time.

Do the AI tools work?

Not yet. Upscaling, background removal and restoration are marked “Not connected” until a real server-side AI service is attached.

Is VELORA free?

Yes. There are no plans, payments or paywalls.
